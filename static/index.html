How this app works

Every control explains itself on hover. This is the short version; the full guide is docs/GUIDE.md.

1 · Get drafts

  1. Describe the image in a sentence or two and pick one or more models (greyed chips are not installed in Draw Things). A negative prompt box appears when a selected model reads one; it is optional, and what you type replaces the default.
  2. Pick a shape. The line under the buttons says the size each selected model will return.
  3. Enrich prompt writes several prompt variants; edit them freely, × drops one. variation: explore varies the composition, hold composition keeps your framing and varies the detail. With 1 variant × 1 seed your text is used exactly as written.
  4. From image… writes the prompt from an existing picture.
  5. Chips are grouped by dialect, prose and danbooru. A mixed selection gets a block of prompts for each, and every model renders only its own. Cards are badged T1/P1 (or V1 when one kind is on screen) for the prompt they came from, orig for your own text.
  6. Generate drafts queues low-res previews: every prompt × model × seed, your own prompt included unless its orig line under the prompt box says it is left out (after Enrich it is, once this session has drafted it). Drafts pick a direction; they are not exact previews of the final.

2 · Act on any image

Finalize / Finalize+
High resolution, same prompt and seed. The + variant first writes a detailed prompt from the image.
Faithful
img2img from the draft: keeps this exact composition at high resolution.
Steps
The small box beside Finalize sets steps for that one final; empty uses the model's own. ⚙ in the header changes what every draft and final of a model gets.
Edit…
No rectangle: an instruction to the edit model for the whole picture — "make her smile", "change the dress to red". Name the target so it can only be one thing, and never as a negation. Comes back at the edit model's working size. Another take on it re-renders this image's prompt at 0.8 instead. With a rectangle: Change what's there repaints inside it in seconds; word it like a prompt (black cat, not make the cat black). Add something new places a new thing inside it, minutes; draw the rectangle around all the space it needs. Nothing outside the rectangle changes. Improve with LLM writes the text for whichever route is selected.
More like this
Same prompt, fresh seeds.
Upscale vs 2x fast
Upscale enlarges and repaints real detail, to 2K, 3K or 4K (minutes). 2x fast is Real-ESRGAN sharpening (seconds, invents nothing, repeatable).
Combine…
Puts what one picture shows into another's scene. Pick two with ⧉, first the subject, second the scene (⇄ swaps), and say what to take and where it goes. A new pose needs its own sentence. About two minutes; Upscale after.
Animate…
A short clip with this image as the first frame. Describe one simple continuous motion the frame supports ("hair drifts in the wind", "slow push in"); camera moves and ambient motion work, a pose change does not. Do not mention the camera unless it should move. Improve with LLM rewrites your note; pick qwen3-vl:8b so it reads the frame. Tens of minutes per clip.
Fix faces
Detects faces and re-renders each; only the face is written back. Strength sets how far it may change. The detector needs eyes, so for a mask or visor drag a box around the face yourself.
Finder
Opens Finder with this image's file selected.

3 · Size and shape

An Edit with no rectangle and Combine come back at the model's working size — edit small, then Upscale. A rectangle, Fix faces and 2x fast leave everything outside them at full resolution. Animate renders at the video model's frame size, so upscaling first buys nothing.

The shape you pick follows the chain: drafts, Finalize, Faithful and Upscale render in it; Edit, Fix faces and 2x fast keep the picture's own. A model without a measured shape renders square.

4 · Stay oriented

  • The show row above the grid filters by kind, model or size; Review ▶ walks what is left one image at a time (→/Space, ←, Esc). Clicking any image starts the review there.
  • Badges show the operation, the prompt variant and the generation time.
  • ⤴ from jumps to the image this one was made from; i shows all settings and the chain back to the original.
  • ✕ on a card deletes the image and its file. Retry appears on failures. Check storage in ⚙ finds cards and files that have lost each other.
  • Remove drafts in ⚙ clears the open session's drafts; one something was made from keeps its prompt and seed so the chain still works.
  • Sessions save automatically; switch from the picker above, ✎ renames the open one.

Models

Untick a model to take it out of the composer, the Edit and Upscale pickers and Animate. That is housekeeping for a crowded row, not a restriction: images you already made with it keep every one of their buttons, and its own Upscale still runs through it. Everything is ticked to begin with.

Denoising steps for every draft and every final this model renders, from now on. The shipped numbers are measured per model — most of them are the point where more steps stopped changing the picture, and a few models get worse with more — so treat them as the considered answer and change one when your own eyes disagree. Steps cost time roughly in proportion, and drafts are the ones you make dozens of. Blank or reset puts a value back to what the model ships with; a changed number is shown in blue.

A single final can be given more steps without changing anything here: type a number in the small box beside Finalize on the card.

Storage

Deleting a card with ✕ already takes its image file with it, so this is for the disagreements that outlive a crash, a failed render or a hand-edited folder: a card with no image left, and an image file no card owns. A card counts once it has finished, whether it succeeded or failed — a failed one will never show a picture either, so this is where those go. Anything still queued or rendering is never touched.

Images are kept in…

Once the drafts have been picked from, Remove drafts deletes the open session's drafts, and only that session's. A draft that a final, a faithful or another draft was made from keeps its prompt, seed and model but not its image, so the lineage of what you kept still leads back to it. Drafts a queued render still needs are left alone.
